Development provenance:
Early scan-reconstruction experiments used LiteReality-Agent (Apache-2.0).
The independent project migrates task-specific room-transfer scripts developed during
that exploration, and replaces its settings, registry and imported inspection helpers.
No LiteReality-Agent Python module is imported by the independent runtime.
This statement does not assert that all algorithms or design ideas are novel.
Historical scripts are retained privately in audit/ as provenance, not as runtime code.
